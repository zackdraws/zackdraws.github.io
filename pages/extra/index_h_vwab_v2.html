Zack Johnson - Animator and Storyboard Artist

Zack Johnson

is a animator, storyboard artist and designer. In 2020 he earned his BFA in character animation from the Laguna College of Art and Design. Before that he attended the Animation Portfolio Workshop in Toronto. Since then he has worked as a freelance artist for New Vision Entertainment, Moving Colour, Deser Mayhem Overdrive as well as teaching animation and design. For job inquiries or any questions he can be reached at ZackJohnsonArt@gmail.com
